How the implied value is worked out

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Category multiple: the upper median of market-cap / TVL over the live tokens of the protocol's own category (at least 3 live tokens; fewer, and the global median is used). Clamped to 0.05 – 1.5.
  • Growth: 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +150%) / 100 × 0.6.
  • Maturity: 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4 — older and still pre-token counts for more; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chains: 1 + 0.02 × min(chains, 5).

The model is computed at runtime from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, seven-day growth, age, chains — never from a hand-kept list of names. A protocol's implied value is what its token would be worth if its record stayed as it is; it does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
